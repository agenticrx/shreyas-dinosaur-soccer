READING LAB

The Fossil Field

Read carefully. Great readers collect evidence.

When Maya joined the fossil club, she expected to spend most afternoons brushing dirt from old rocks. Instead, her first field trip took the team to a dry hillside where tiny clues were hidden everywhere.

Maya noticed three curved marks beside a flat stone. The club leader explained that scientists do not guess from one clue. They compare evidence, ask questions, and record what they observe. Maya sketched the marks and labeled the page “evidence first.”

By the end of the afternoon, Maya had not found a complete fossil. She had found something better: a new way to think. The next morning, she returned to her notebook with three questions and a plan to test each one.
